How a book gets made

Three steps on your side. You steer the story; our writing tool drafts it, and nothing prints until you have read every page.

01

Tell us about them

A name, an age, what they look like and what they love. Five minutes, no account needed.

02

Read it, change it

You get the whole book back to read. Rewrite a line, swap an illustration, redo the dedication — as many times as you like.

03

We print and bind it

Printed on 150gsm uncoated stock, cased by hand, posted in a protective slip within five days.
